Controller and contact

The controller for this website is the operator named above. For privacy questions or rights requests, email devzerviate@proton.me.

Hosting and technical request data

This site is a static website served by a Cloudflare Worker at dev.zerviates.net. When you open it, Cloudflare processes your IP address, request time and URL, browser information, response codes and security-related request data to deliver the site, troubleshoot failures and protect it from abuse. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in a reliable and secure website.

Cloudflare's dashboard shows me aggregated traffic statistics and, for requests its security features block or challenge, event details such as IP address and URL. I have no access to individual request logs, and no Cloudflare analytics feature is enabled for this site. Cloudflare keeps request data according to its own retention practices.

Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA, and its service providers may process data outside the EEA. Cloudflare processes this data on my behalf under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes standard contractual clauses for international transfers. See also Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Local settings and browser features

The site does not set cookies, use analytics or advertising trackers, or embed third-party content. Fonts, images and scripts are served from the site itself, so viewing the covers does not contact TMDB or IGDB.

Only when you switch the theme, the choice is saved in your browser under jr-portfolio:theme. It stays until you clear the site's local storage and is never transmitted. This storage provides the appearance preference you explicitly request (§ 25(2)(2) TDDDG). Theme switching still works for the current page if storage is unavailable.

Copying the contact address writes to your clipboard only after you press the copy button; the site never reads your clipboard.

Email and external links

If you email me, I process your address, message and any details you choose to include to respond. My mailbox is provided by Proton AG in Switzerland; your own email provider also handles the message. Depending on the request, the basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual correspondence or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for responding to other inquiries. See Proton's privacy policy.

I delete correspondence once your inquiry is resolved, unless a statutory retention obligation requires keeping it longer. Providing information is voluntary, but I need a reply address and enough context to answer.

External links open independently operated services. Their privacy notices apply after you navigate there. This site asks browsers not to send the referring page's URL.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR's conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability (Articles 15–20). Under Article 21, you may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your situation.

Under Article 77, you may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live, work or where you believe the infringement took place. The site makes no automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects.
